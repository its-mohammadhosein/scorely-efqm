FAQ

Questions
we hear a lot.

Is the score official?

No. Scorely gives you a structured self-assessment score using the EFQM Model's criteria, weights and RADAR logic. Official recognition only comes from an assessment by EFQM or its partners — Scorely helps you prepare for it.

Do we need EFQM-trained people to use it?

It helps to have one trained assessor to lead the process. Everyone else just answers plain questions about their area, with guidance on what good looks like.

Can we import last year's assessment?

Yes. Upload your previous scores from a spreadsheet and Scorely maps them to the criteria, so you can compare from day one.

Is our data safe?

Your assessment stays private to your company. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Enterprise customers can host Scorely on their own servers.

Does it work in Persian?

Yes. The whole app and every report work in English and Persian, with a proper right-to-left layout.
